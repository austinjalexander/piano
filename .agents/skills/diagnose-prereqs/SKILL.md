# Diagnose a starting point

Read `GOALS.md`, `PROGRESS.md`, `docs/CURRICULUM.md`, and `docs/agents/tutoring.md`. Use `assessments/baseline.md` for initial placement, or a narrower probe for the current passage.

Ask only tasks relevant to the next lesson. Distinguish unfamiliar, not attempted, assisted, self-reported, and directly reviewed work. Do not require a new player to attempt hands-together playing or repertoire they have never learned. When no readable score is available, leave staff reading unassessed.

Record actual strengths, gaps, evidence limits, and one next task in a dated assessment/practice record. A diagnostic can support skipping unnecessary teaching; it does not automatically check earlier topics or assign an exam grade. Adjust the learning order from evidence without inventing a background or practice budget.
