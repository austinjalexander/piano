# Review a piano attempt

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agents/tutoring.md`, the actual task/conditions, its rubric after the attempt, and the submitted evidence. Check whether the source is accessible before claiming to inspect it.

Separate pitch, rhythm, continuity, fingering/movement where visible, and musical intention. Identify one supported obstacle and a small corrective task. Do not infer sound from text or posture from audio. Label self-reported results, direct observations, and teacher reports distinctly.

Record assistance, tempo and beat unit, actual duration or unknown, result per criterion, and evidence path. Retain an unsuccessful or assisted attempt and arrange another independent check when needed. Use the later-day review and learner-authored reflection gate before updating topic completion. A single polished phrase does not complete a phase or prove fresh-reading skill.
