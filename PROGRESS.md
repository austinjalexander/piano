## How to update

Use the criteria in [tutoring guidance](docs/agents/tutoring.md). A checked topic needs corrected required practice, its stated independent playing/knowledge check, a successful review on another day, and a dated learner-authored reflection. Link evidence and label it self-reported, audio-reviewed, video-reviewed, or teacher-reported. Text alone does not establish observed tone, posture, or timing.

Record partial work without checking the box. Practice minutes, authored lessons, video viewing, and provider completion are separate from demonstrated skill. Later topics need authored tasks and rubrics before a completion decision.
