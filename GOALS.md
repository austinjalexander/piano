## Working learning goals

These are starting proposals to refine with the learner:

- Build a sustainable practice habit and comfortable keyboard familiarity.
- Read simple music in both clefs and keep a steady pulse.
- Coordinate both hands, recognize common keys and chords, and connect theory with sound.
- Learn personally meaningful pieces and keep a small playable repertoire.
- Develop listening, sight-reading, playing by ear, and simple improvisation alongside repertoire.
- Learn how to diagnose a difficult passage and practice it effectively.
