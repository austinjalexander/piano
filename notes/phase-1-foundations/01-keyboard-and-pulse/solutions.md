# Keyboard and pulse corrections and rubric

Use after an attempt. Close this file during the independent check. The check prompt and exact pass criteria live in [the README](README.md#independent-check); do not silently change them during grading.

## Practice corrections

1. **Keyboard map:** C lies immediately before a two-black-key group; D is between its two black keys; E immediately follows. F lies before a three-black-key group; G and A occupy its gaps; B follows. **Check:** moving right gives C D E F G A B C repeatedly.
2. **Recall:** G is between the first and second black keys of a three-key group; C precedes a two-key group; A lies between the second and third of a three-key group; F precedes a three-key group; D lies within a two-key group; B follows a three-key group; E follows a two-key group. **Check:** verify each against a different nearby group, not just a memorized key position.
3. **Fingers:** thumb 1, index 2, middle 3, ring 4, little 5 on either hand. **Check:** RH C-to-G uses 1 2 3 4 5; LH C-to-G uses 5 4 3 2 1 in the starting position.
4. **Pulse:** all eight counts are equally spaced; claps on 1 and 3 occur two beats apart. **Check:** counting continues during counts 2 and 4 even though there is no clap.
5. **Phrase preparation:** RH fingering is 1 2 3 5 | 3 2 1; LH is 5 4 3 1 | 3 4 5. The final C is held across counts 3–4. **Check:** seven key presses occupy eight beats, with no second press on the final beat.
6. **Continuous phrase:** listen for the E-to-G move, the bar transition G-to-E, and the final two-beat hold. **Check:** both bars fit eight beats after the lead-in; hands are assessed separately.

## Feedback and evidence

Record knowledge, RH playing, and LH playing separately. If only one component passes, retain that evidence and target the remaining gap. An assisted correction belongs in practice, followed by a new independent attempt.

An audio recording can support pitch, rhythm, and continuity feedback when accessible to the reviewer. It cannot show posture or finger choice. Video may support visible movement/fingering; label what can actually be seen. A learner's log can support a self-reported result but must never be described as an observed performance.

The later review uses the same phrase on another day, with the same conditions. The learner writes the reflection; do not generate their personal account. Mark P1.1 complete only when all required practice, initial check, later review, and reflection evidence are linked.
