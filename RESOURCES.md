## Short theory and listening supplements

- [musictheory.net lessons](https://www.musictheory.net/lessons/): staff/clefs, durations, measures, rests, and later scales/chords. Pair a selected concept with playing it at the keyboard.
- [musictheory.net exercises](https://www.musictheory.net/exercises): keyboard/staff identification and ear-training exercises. Choose a small note range or interval set matching the lesson; record the task settings and actual results separately from playing evidence.

Both pages offer free browser activities. No paid app or subscription is needed for this starting path.
