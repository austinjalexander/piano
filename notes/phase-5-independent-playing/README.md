# Phase 5 Independent playing

Use the relevant reading, coordination, listening, and harmony prerequisites for each task. Small creative activities can start much earlier; independence grows through repeated practice decisions.

- **P5.1 Learn a new piece:** choose an accessible arrangement, map its sections, isolate difficult passages, and evaluate a practice plan.
- **P5.2 Ear and creativity:** recover a short melody by ear, vary it, and improvise over a familiar chord pattern.
- **P5.3 Share and maintain:** prepare a short set, record or share it if desired, reflect, and revisit selected repertoire.

This is a scope outline; detailed projects and checks are pending. Adapt musical styles and sharing expectations to the learner's goals. Define the actual piece, excerpt, preparation, and success criteria before assessing.
