# Keyboard and pulse exercises

Complete tasks 1–6 over short sessions. Record actual attempts, corrections, assistance, and one observation in [a practice log](../../../templates/practice-log-template.md). Consult notes during practice; close them for the independent check.

1. **Keyboard map:** find three different groups of two black keys. Locate C, D, and E beside each. Repeat with three-black-key groups to locate F, G, A, and B. Use fewer groups on a short keyboard and describe the available range.
2. **Recall:** look away, then find the following white keys in any comfortable register: G, C, A, F, D, B, E. Explain one location using its neighboring black-key group. Check and correct any uncertain note.
3. **Finger numbers:** point to each finger and say its number on both hands. Place a hand over five neighboring white keys without pressing; release it and repeat with the other hand.
4. **Pulse:** count two groups of four aloud and clap once per beat. Then clap on 1 and 3 while counting all four beats. Record whether the count stayed even. Use a slow click if helpful and log the setting.
5. **Phrase preparation:** use the original phrase in [the notes](notes.md). Play the pitches with RH, then LH, with no tempo demand. Fix the move from E to G. Next play just the second bar, holding the final C for two counts.
6. **Continuous phrase:** count a four-beat lead-in and play both bars with RH; then do the same with LH separately. Try two stable practice performances per hand, pausing between tries. Log actual results rather than only the best try.

**Optional variation:** make a new two-bar phrase using only C, D, E, and G with four beats per bar. Play it or sing its contour. This is creative practice, not part of the mastery gate.

When ready, use the [independent check](README.md#independent-check). After a successful check, review the phrase on another day and add your own reflection before requesting a progress update.
