Start here if piano is new or the [baseline](../../assessments/baseline.md) reveals gaps. Practice keyboard geography, counting, reading landmarks, and listening in small connected tasks.

- **P1.1:** [Keyboard and pulse](01-keyboard-and-pulse/README.md) — complete starter lesson, exercises, and independent check.
- **P1.2:** rhythm and rests — scope in [the curriculum](../../docs/CURRICULUM.md); detailed lesson pending.
- **P1.3:** grand staff landmarks — scope in the curriculum; detailed lesson pending.
- **P1.4:** listening and echoing — scope in the curriculum; detailed lesson pending.

Finish topic checks using [the evidence policy](../../docs/agents/tutoring.md), then move toward [reading and coordination](../phase-2-reading-and-coordination/README.md). A preliminary diagnostic can guide placement without completing these topics.
