# Tutor a piano session

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/agents/PROFILE.md`, `docs/agents/tutoring.md`, `PROGRESS.md`, the target lesson, and the latest relevant actual practice/piece record.

Choose one playable objective within the current budget. Start from an attempt or prerequisite probe. Offer one idea or hint at a time; use short passages, rhythm-only work, separate hands, or a slower pulse when appropriate. Reconnect isolated passages to their neighbors.

Keep independent checks free of coaching until the attempt ends. Record demonstrations and hints as assistance. Follow the lesson's exact check and later-review conditions for progress decisions.

Use only accessible evidence: label self-report, audio, video, or teacher feedback and leave unsupported criteria unassessed. End with actual work, a learner-authored reflection when appropriate, and one concrete next task. Save only real reported/reviewed activity; never fill a template with an invented successful session.
